The road divides beneath an old stone marker. Rain has softened the names carved into its three faces, but the paths themselves remain clear.

The eastern road descends towards a line of distant lights. The western road disappears beneath dark trees. A narrow track continues north across the open heath.

oa. Take the eastern road.
ob. Follow the road into the trees.
oc. Continue north across the heath.
od. Remain at the crossroads for a while longer.
