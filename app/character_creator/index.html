Core Choices

Choose the broad identity first. Hover or focus any option for a plain-language explanation.

Ability Scores

The standard array is assigned from class priorities by default. Changing one score swaps it with the ability currently holding that number.

Other Choices

These are extra choices created by the species, class, subclass, or feat you selected. They stay visible after completion so they can be changed.

Advancement Feats

At supported advancement levels, choose either Ability Score Improvement or one available feat. Feat subchoices appear directly beneath the feat.

Spells

Choose class spells here. Spells granted by species or feats are marked as granted and do not count against class spell choices.

Gear

Select starting combat gear. Weapon choices can unlock follow-up choices such as weapon mastery or subclass weapon bonds.
