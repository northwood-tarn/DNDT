# Canonical Merchant Roster

This document fixes the campaign's merchant locations and their broad stock roles. It is the definitive merchant roster unless the campaign structure is deliberately revised.

Exact inventories, prices, merchant names, dialogue, special items and item-unlock flags will be authored later.

## System Rules

- Shopping takes place through dialogue.
- Closing a shop returns the player to the associated dialogue.
- Shops may hide individual offers until required story flags are set.
- Limited stock and sold-out unique items persist in the save.
- Any merchant buys any ordinarily sellable item. Merchants do not restrict purchases by item category.
- Quest items, keys and items explicitly marked unsellable cannot be sold.
- Sold items disappear rather than entering the merchant's stock.
- Purchase prices are fixed by the shop inventory.
- The resale percentage is `50 + Charisma modifier + Persuasion proficiency bonus + background bonus`.
- The resale percentage has a minimum of 40% and maximum of 70%.
- Artisan, Charlatan, Criminal and Merchant backgrounds receive a +4 background bonus.

## Act I: Greyharbour

Act I contains four merchants.

### 1. Dockside Chandler and Provisioner

Location: Greyharbour docks.

Broad stock role:

- General exploration supplies
- Rope and practical dock or travel equipment
- Basic consumables and remedies
- Basic healing potions
- Ammunition
- Cheap, ordinary weapons
- A limited selection of light armour

### 2. Greyharbour Armourer or Weaponsmith

Location: Greyharbour proper.

Broad stock role:

- Mundane weapons
- Shields
- Light and medium armour
- A limited selection of heavy armour
- Ammunition
- Martial consumables, where applicable

The blockade may be represented through limited stock without changing the shop's basic role.

### 3. Refinery Alchemist or Industrial Supplier

Location: the Refinery.

Broad stock role:

- Acid and alchemical fire
- Antitoxin and remedies
- Healing potions
- Oil- and light-related equipment
- Fire-resistant or protective consumables
- Technical tools and devices, where applicable

Individual offers may be unlocked as the refinery situation changes.

### 4. Deep Library Scroll and Bookseller

Location: the Deep Library section of Greyharbour.

Broad stock role:

- Spell scrolls
- Magical and scholarly books
- Ritual or research materials
- Arcane utility consumables
- Magical texts and reference works

## Act II: Necropolis

Act II contains four merchants.

### 5. Wall-Market Outfitter

Location: Inside the Walls, near the party's initial route into the Necropolis.

Broad stock role:

- Expedition supplies
- Healing and defensive consumables
- Ammunition
- Improved mundane weapons
- Improved armour
- Necrotic-protection equipment
- Equipment suited to prisons, ruins and ossuaries

### 6. Twilight Bazaar Arms and Relic Dealer

Location: the Twilight Bazaar.

Broad stock role:

- Advanced weapons and armour
- Magical weapons and protective equipment
- Necropolis-made gear
- Relics
- Class-oriented equipment

### 7. Twilight Bazaar Apothecary and Occult Supplier

Location: the Twilight Bazaar.

Broad stock role:

- Stronger healing potions
- Antitoxins and condition remedies
- Alchemical weapons
- Necrotic and magical protection
- Spell-related consumables
- Occult utility items

### 8. Chalk Residences Collector

Location: the Chalk Residences.

This NPC is a private collector rather than an ordinary retailer.

Broad stock role:

- Curios
- Rare equipment
- Historical or personal objects
- Unusual magical items

Some or most offers begin hidden. Dialogue and Persuasion outcomes set flags that reveal the items the collector has agreed to sell.

## Act III: Backlands

Act III contains two merchants. They are isolated individuals rather than evidence of functioning settlements or ordinary commercial infrastructure.

### 9. Untended Graves Scavenger

Location: the Untended Graves.

Broad stock role:

- Scavenged weapons
- Incense
- Healing and defensive consumables
- Usable grave goods
- Equipment recovered from failed travellers

The inventory should feel incomplete and opportunistic rather than comprehensive.

### 10. Approach to the Portal Arcane Merchant

Location: on top of the Escarpment of Eyes or on the ascent toward the Portal.

This is the final planned purchasing opportunity before the endgame approach.

Broad stock role:

- Arcane items
- Strong consumables
- Spell scrolls
- Magical protection
- Late-game utility items
- A limited selection of expensive permanent items

## Areas Without Dedicated Merchants

- No-Man's Land
- The Regnant Eternal
- The Administrative Enclave
- The Endless Plain outside the specified merchant locations
- The Escarpment of Eyes outside the Portal approach merchant
- Towards the Portal after the final merchant

The Chalk Residences have only the collector. Other NPCs in these areas may provide dialogue or non-shopping services without becoming additional merchants.
