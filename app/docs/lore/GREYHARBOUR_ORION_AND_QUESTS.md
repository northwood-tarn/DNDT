# Grey Harbour: Orion and Initial Quests

Status: locked canonical campaign information.

## Orion

At this point in the campaign, Grey Harbour is led by Orion. She is a hefty former fisherwoman in her sixties, with short grey hair and a scar that crosses her forehead and cuts into the top of her hairline. Her arms retain the strength of her former work.

Orion deeply loves and cares for Grey Harbour. Because she is frightened for it, she presents a calm, stoic, capable face over profound anxiety and concern.

Her motives were transformed by the collapse of the Outer Harbour: the harbour buildings and urban district that once abutted the Necropolis and have been destroyed over the past couple of years. Orion outwardly acts and leads as she did before, but the loss broke her. She now continues organising Grey Harbour while, privately, stoically organising for its death.

The older Veyra Soll material describes an earlier campaign concept or earlier Greyharbour history. It does not replace Orion as the leader at this point in the campaign.

When the party approaches Orion, she offers the following quests. She says someone of their “broader skill set” may be able to make progress.

## An Illuminated Manuscript

Proceed to the Library of the Deep. Its main arcane scholar, Twombly, has been missing for a couple of days. Before disappearing, he reported finding something that might be useful.

No culprit, reward, outcome, or further quest stage is fixed yet.

## Oil and Water

Go to the oil refinery, where certain oily fish are turned into Lanterna oil, and investigate rumours that the oil has become contaminated.

No source of contamination, culprit, reward, outcome, or further quest stage is fixed yet.
